# 第一关海边风格探索 v1

## 参考图用法

- 队友海岸图：只取蓝绿海水、暖沙色、柔和绿色的配色与海边氛围；不复用它的岩石构图、门、钥匙、鸟或人物。
- 《悠悠长假》场景拼图：只取手绘轮廓、概括形状和动画背景的绘制语言；不复用具体建筑与构图。

## 生成提示词

```text
Create one original 16:9 environment concept image for a 2D horizontal side-scrolling platform game, a quiet South China seaside childhood setting. Use reference image 1 ONLY for its restful palette: clear cyan-blue sky and sea, muted blue-green coast, warm pale sand, soft olive and leaf greens. Do not copy its layout, cliffs, door, key, birds, figures, or dense rock obstacles. Use reference image 2 ONLY for its drawing language: hand-inked French children's animation background, slightly irregular dark umber outlines, simplified painted shapes, restrained gouache/watercolor brush texture, visible but sparse hand-made strokes. Avoid glossy digital painting, photorealistic detail, 3D-rendered lighting, and excessive texture.

Camera and composition: strict side-on 2D platform game view, not a deep perspective landscape, not isometric. Compose a broad horizontal coast with a calm sky occupying the upper third, a clean readable sea band and distant low island silhouettes, a simple middle-distance shoreline with a few small Lingnan coastal houses and wind-shaped trees, and a continuous open sandy/earthy gameplay path along the bottom. Keep the walkable ground line level and uninterrupted across the full width; only tiny grass clumps and a few low stones at the very edges. The middle and foreground must leave generous clear negative space for a small running child to read against the background. Separate shapes by depth and value so a game artist could later divide the image into far sky/islands, sea, midground coast, and foreground edge. Gentle late-morning daylight, quiet warm everyday mood, comfortable low-to-medium saturation, broad simple color masses, details concentrated sparingly around houses and shoreline.

No character, no foreground boulders, no rocks or ledges blocking the path, no bridge, no doorway, no collectible items, no text, no frame, no collage.
```

## 后续拆分方向

本图是风格和构图探索，不是游戏内的最终单张底图。正式资产应拆为远天空/岛屿、海面、海岸中景、前景地面与装饰层，保留可走路线并分别调视差。
