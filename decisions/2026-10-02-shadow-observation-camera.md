## Decision: Use a frontal observation camera and a fixed flashlight cradle
## Context: The wide room perspective, independently scaled props and painting produced disconnected shadows and ambiguous dragging.
## Alternatives considered: Continue fitting the painting into the wide background; create a dedicated frontal observation set.
## Reasoning: A frontal wall and tabletop establish one receiving surface. Objects sit on a transparent prop board halfway between lamp and wall. One ray projection controls shadow placement; the lamp switch gates illumination and shadows. The original painting assets remain unchanged.
## Trade-offs accepted: The observation set currently uses simple procedural wall and table materials; it still needs authored environment art to match the illustrated room. Lamp movement is replaced by an on/off switch; puzzle difficulty comes from arranging and rotating the three objects. Existing piece coordinates and callbacks remain compatible.
